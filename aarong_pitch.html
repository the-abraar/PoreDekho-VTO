Implementation Plan

Live on Aarong.com in
Under 30 Days.

Our pipeline is designed to move fast. We've done the R&D — you just need to say go.

Week 1
Catalogue Audit & Asset Collection
We work with your product team to select the first 10–25 SKUs for digitisation. High-res reference photos + product specs shared via Drive.
Week 2
3D Model Creation & Optimisation
Our 3D artists build lightweight GLB models (≤ 4MB each) with accurate PBR materials to replicate gold, brass, and stone finishes. Each model is tested on mid-range Bangladeshi Android devices.
Week 3
Integration & QA
We integrate the <model-viewer> component into Aarong's product detail pages (or provide an embeddable widget for your dev team). We test across iOS Safari Quick Look and Android WebXR.
