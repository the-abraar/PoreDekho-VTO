## Key Value Propositions (The "Why Us")
1. **Zero Friction:** No app downloads required. Works instantly on Facebook, Instagram, or your website.
2. **Instant Trust:** Bridges the gap between viewing a flat 2D image and knowing exactly how the jewelry/accessory looks on the customer.
3. **Data-Driven Sales:** Customers who use AR are 40% more likely to convert and 30% less likely to return items (RTO).

## Target Personas
* **The High-End Jeweler:** Cares about brand prestige. Wants their digital presence to feel as luxurious as their physical store.
* **The Boutique Owner:** Cares about reducing costs. RTO (Returns) eat into their margins. They need a tool to make sure customers keep what they buy.
* **The F-Commerce Hustler:** Runs a massive Facebook page. Needs viral marketing. A VTO filter gives their customers a reason to share photos and tag the shop.
