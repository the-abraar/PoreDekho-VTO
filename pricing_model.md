# Pricing Strategy: "Startup" Model

To cater to the price-sensitive Bangladeshi market, we avoid massive upfront enterprise fees. Instead, we use a hybrid model:

## 1. The Setup Fee (One-Time)
* **Price:** ৳10,000 - ৳25,000 (BDT)
* **Includes:** Creation of initial 3D models (depending on the number of products) and integration into Facebook/Instagram or website.

## 2. The "Collection" Fee (Recurring Service)
* **Price:** Tiered based on volume.
* **Concept:** A smaller fee charged every time the brand launches a new seasonal collection (e.g., "The Puja Collection", "The Eid Collection", or "Wedding Season").

## 3. The Performance Bonus (Advanced/Partnership)
* **Concept:** For larger brands with high traffic, offer a significantly lower setup fee in exchange for a small commission on sales tracked through the VTO affiliate link or promo code.
* **Benefit:** Reduces friction for adoption and aligns our incentives with their sales growth.
