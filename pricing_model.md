# Pricing Strategy: "Enterprise Growth" Model

To cater to the Bangladeshi B2B market—balancing the need for accessible entry points with premium, scalable enterprise services—we employ a value-based, hybrid pricing model. This reduces initial friction while ensuring long-term partnership value.

## 1. The Integration & Asset Creation Fee (One-Time Setup)
* **Price:** ৳15,000 - ৳40,000 (BDT) [Tiered based on catalog complexity]
* **Includes:** 
    * 3D modeling and AR asset optimization for an initial batch of flagship products (e.g., 5-15 items).
    * Seamless integration into the brand's Facebook Page, Instagram, or custom E-commerce website.
    * Initial onboarding, QA testing, and tech support.
* **Value Prop:** A low-risk investment to modernize their storefront and immediately test the ROI of AR technology.

## 2. The Seasonal Collection Subscription (Recurring)
* **Price:** ৳10,000 - ৳25,000 (BDT) per new collection launch.
* **Concept:** Instead of a rigid monthly SaaS fee (which Bangladeshi SMEs often resist), we charge a retainer or per-collection fee. When a brand launches a "Wedding Season," "Eid Collection," or "Summer Line," we digitize the new catalog.
* **Value Prop:** Aligns our billing directly with the client's revenue-generating events. They only pay when they have new products to push.

## 3. The Enterprise Performance Partnership (For High-Volume Brands)
* **Concept:** For top-tier brands with massive daily traffic, we offer a significantly subsidized setup fee in exchange for a performance-based revenue share or a usage-based tier (e.g., cost per 1,000 virtual try-ons).
* **Value Prop:** "We win when you win." This model completely removes the barrier to entry for large brands and aligns our incentives with their direct sales growth and RTO reduction. 

## Add-On Services (Upsells)
* **Custom AR Mini-Games / Filters:** For viral brand awareness campaigns (e.g., Catch the jewelry to win a discount code).
* **Advanced Analytics Dashboard:** Monthly reports detailing which items are tried on the most, average try-on time, and conversion correlation.
