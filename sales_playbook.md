# PoreDekho VTO Sales Playbook - Bangladesh Market

## 1. Strategy for Identifying High-Value Prospects

To close deals with jewelry brands and F-commerce boutiques in Bangladesh, we need to target businesses that already have traction but are looking to scale or differentiate. Focus on Facebook and Instagram, as these are the primary channels for F-commerce in BD.

**Key Indicators of a High-Value Page:**
*   **Engagement Rate, Not Just Followers:** Look for pages with active comments and shares on their posts, especially "live" sessions. A page with 50k followers but dead comments is worth less than a page with 15k followers and hundreds of comments asking for prices ("Inbox please", "Price?").
*   **High-Quality Visuals:** Brands that invest in professional photography or high-quality video content for their jewelry are prime targets. They already understand the value of visual presentation.
*   **Frequent Facebook Live Sessions:** Many BD boutiques sell primarily through Facebook Live. These brands desperately need a way for customers to visualize the product outside of the live stream to reduce return rates and increase off-hours sales.
*   **Product Categories:** Focus on statement pieces, bridal jewelry (heavy sets), and premium imitation or gold-plated jewelry. High-ticket items benefit the most from Virtual Try-On.
*   **Customer Pain Points in Comments:** Look for comments like "Can you show how it looks when worn?", "Is the size adjustable?", or frequent complaints about size/fit after delivery.

**Where to Search:**
*   Facebook Search: "Jewelry Bangladesh", "Boutique Dhaka", "Bridal Jewelry BD".
*   Instagram Hashtags: #bdjewelry #dhakaboutique #bangladeshiwedding #deshijewelry.
*   Lookalike Pages: Check the "Related Pages" on Facebook for successful brands you already know.

## 3. Handling the "It's too expensive" Objection

**Context in BD Market:** Budget sensitivity is high, and SaaS/recurring software costs are often viewed as expenses rather than investments. We must shift the focus from "cost" to "ROI" and emphasize the risk-free nature of the performance model.

**Prospect:** *"Your setup fee is too high / Software subscription is too expensive for us right now."*

**Response Strategy (The Pivot to Value):**
1.  **Acknowledge and Validate:** "I completely understand. Cost is always a major factor, especially right now." / *"Ami ekdom bujhte parchi. Cost ta asholei ekta boro bishoy ekhon."*
2.  **Refocus on the Performance Model (Risk Reversal):**
    *   **English:** "That's exactly why we introduced the 'Setup + Performance Bonus' model for our BD partners. We only take a minimum to cover the technical integration of putting your 3D models online. The real profit for us only comes *after* you make extra sales through the VTO feature. If you don't make more money, we don't either."
    *   **Benglish:** "Thik ei karonei amra BD partner der jonno 'Setup + Performance Bonus' model ta enechi. Setup fee ta khub e minimal, just apnader product guloke 3D te convert kore integrate korar khoroch. Amader ashol profit ta hobe tokhon-i, jokhon ei VTO use kore apnader extra sale hobe. Apnar sale na barle amader kono bonus e dite hobe na."
3.  **The ROI Question:**
    *   **English:** "If this tool helps you convert just 5 more high-ticket bridal sets a month because customers felt confident trying it on virtually, wouldn't the setup fee pay for itself in the first couple of weeks?"
    *   **Benglish:** "Jodi amader ei tool ta apnake mashe extra 5 ta bridal set beshi sell korte help kore (karon kustomer ra try on kore confident feel korche), tahole ki setup fee ta prothom 2-1 soptah tei uthe ashbe na?"
4.  **The Downgrade/Trial Close:**
    *   **English:** "How about this: Let's start with just your top 10 best-selling items instead of the whole catalog. That keeps the initial setup extremely low, and we can prove the ROI before you scale it to the rest of the store. Sound fair?"
    *   **Benglish:** "Tahole ekta kaaj kori: Pura catalog er bodole, apnader top 10 ta best-selling item diye start kori. Ete initial setup ta ekdom kom hobe, ar amrao apnake result ta proman kore dekhate parbo puro store e apply korar age. Kemon hoy?"
