# Free pilot: terms (EN + BN)

Send this after a seller says yes. Plain terms, no signature needed for a free pilot.

## English

1. **What:** we make a try-on link for **5** of your earrings or necklaces. Customers open it on their phone, see the piece on their own face, and order on WhatsApp or Messenger.
2. **How long:** **4 to 6 weeks** from the day the first link goes live.
3. **Cost:** **free**. No fee now, and no fee is owed when the pilot ends. Prices, if any, come later and only if you want to continue.
4. **What you give:** photos of the 5 pieces (see `sku_photo_checklist.md`) and each piece's real width in mm. Your WhatsApp number or Messenger link for the order button.
5. **Data we ask for:** once a week, two columns from your order sheet: the order code and whether it was **delivered** or **returned**. Try-on orders carry a code starting `PD-`, so we can compare their return rate with your other orders. No customer names, phone numbers or addresses, ever.
6. **Privacy:** the camera image never leaves the customer's phone. We do not see or store faces.
7. **Your photos:** stay yours. We use them only for your try-on links. We do not name your page anywhere without your written permission.
8. **How to stop:** send "stop" at any time, for any reason. We take the links down within 2 days and delete your photos.
9. **At the end:** you get the result (return rate of try-on orders vs other orders), whatever it shows.
