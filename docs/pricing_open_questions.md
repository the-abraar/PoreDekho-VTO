## Unit-cost questions to answer before setting any price
1. Minutes of human time per SKU (photo, background removal, anchor setting, QA)? Measure over 20+ SKUs.
2. Is the deliverable a 2D overlay or a 3D GLB? Artist/photogrammetry cost per 3D SKU if 3D?
3. Cost of rework and revision rounds per SKU; share of SKUs that fail realism QA.
4. Hosting, bandwidth and CDN cost per shop per month at expected try-on volume.
5. Support and account-management time per shop per month.
6. Payment/collection cost and VAT/tax treatment in BD; USD vs BDT billing.
7. SKUs per seller per year (new collections per Eid/Puja) and expected churn.
8. Seller willingness to pay: interview data, not assumption.
9. Can revenue share be attributed (order-code matching), and at what measurement cost?
10. Target margin multiple over measured cost (judge.md suggests 3x or more).
