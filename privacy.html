Privacy

Camera try-on (tryon.html)

  • We ask before turning on the camera.
  • The camera video is processed in your browser, on your phone. It is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is not stored.
  • A picture leaves your phone only if you tap "Save or share picture" and choose where it goes.
  • To track your face, the page downloads code and model files from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and Google (storage.googleapis.com). Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details. They do not receive your camera video.
  • After the first visit, your browser keeps those code and model files in its cache (about 6 MB) so the try-on starts faster next time. Clearing this site's data removes them.
  • Your placement settings are kept in your browser's local storage, on your phone only.
  • If you tap "Order on WhatsApp" or "Order on Messenger", your message and order code go to the shop through that app, under that app's privacy policy.

Seller tools (admin.html, report.html)

Product photos and order lists you load into these pages are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.

This website

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The demo-request form is not connected, so it sends nothing.
